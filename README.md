# 👑 ' Royal Sleeping Kingdom &  Sloth Laboratory 🎀✨

An unhinged, ultra-cute, posh pink-girly interactive tribute to **Sanvitha (Sanns)** — celebrating her legendary horizontal energy-saving lifestyle, sleep mastery, and supreme friendship!

## ✨ Features
- 👑 **Editorial Aesthetic**: Haute-couture pink design with delicate glassmorphism, champagne gold accents, floating silk sparkles, and refined typography (*Cormorant Garamond*, *Playfair Display*, and *Plus Jakarta Sans*).
- 🚨 **Stage 1 (Breaking News & Sloth Terminal)**: Simulated diagnostic telemetry calculating Sanns' horizontal gravity pull and 18-hour slumber cycles.
- 🔒 **Stage 2 (Consciousness Check)**: Interactive anti-sloth CAPTCHA with funny roasts and a 2-second hold-down motor verification.
- 🧠 **Stage 3 (8 Ridiculous Questions with Right/Wrong Mechanics)**:
  - Wrong answers shake the screen, sound the buzzer, and playfully roast her while letting her guess again.
  - The canonical right answers trigger celebratory chimes, green glowing victory borders, and confetti bursts!
  - Includes the dodging **"Bed vs Bestie"** emergency choice on Question 8 where the Bed button literally runs away from the cursor/finger.
- 🎮 **Stage 4 (3 Posh Mini-Games)**:
  - **Catch Sleepy Hearts**: 15s timed arcade catching boba, hearts, and duvets while dodging alarms.
  - **Wake Sanns Up**: Rapid-alarm spam clicker counteracting her snooze decay.
  - **Catch the Satin Pillow**: High-speed dodging pillow chase.
- 📊 **Stage 5 (Scientific Sloth Dossier)**: Animated counter breakdown of Sleep Affinity (999%), Alarms Ignored (24x), and Bestie Rating (100%).
- 👑 **Stage 6 (Grand Coronation & VIP Coupons)**:
  - Official royal proclamation: *“SANVITHA (SANNS) — THE SUPREME PINK PRINCESS, CEO OF NAPS, DESTROYER OF PRODUCTIVITY, AND CERTIFIED BESTIE.”*
  - Heartfelt letter from her best friend.
  - 4 interactive redeemable friendship privilege coupons.
- 🎀 **Bonus Features**:
  - Web Audio synthesizer with sound effects and mute toggle.
  - Floating hearts cursor trail.
  - 5-tap crown easter egg for royal immunity.

## 🚀 Running Locally
Open `index.html` in any web browser! No dependencies or build steps required.
